### About the Experiment

This mini project demonstrates the concept of **Brewster’s Angle** and its relationship with polarized light. Brewster’s angle is the angle of incidence at which the reflected light becomes completely plane-polarized under ideal conditions.

The project helps users understand the interaction of laser light with a transparent surface, the phenomenon of polarization, and the relationship between Brewster’s angle and the refractive index of a material.

The project provides a virtual demonstration of the experiment to make the underlying physics concepts easier to understand through visualization and interactive learning.
