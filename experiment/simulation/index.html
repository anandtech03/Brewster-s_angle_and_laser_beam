Theory

At Brewster's angle, the reflected p-polarized component vanishes for an ideal dielectric interface. The reflected and refracted rays are perpendicular at this angle.

tan(θB) = n₂ / n₁
n₁ sin(θᵢ) = n₂ sin(θₜ)

Here, θB is Brewster's angle, θᵢ is the angle of incidence, and θₜ is the angle of refraction. Angles are measured from the normal.
